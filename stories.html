Ray of Hope 4.0 — inspiring dreams for a colourful future

Our flagship outreach returned for a fourth edition, working inside a single underprivileged community over several weeks. The programme combined encouragement with practical training, and closed with an exhibition, trade fair and awards ceremony where trainees presented — and sold — what they had made.
