Give

Donate to Chrysolite Foundation

Every naira is spent inside a programme you can read about on this site — a community outreach, a six-week training cohort, or a child's return to school.

Choose an amount

Donate ₦5,000

Secure payments by Flutterwave — card, bank transfer and USSD. No account required. You will be taken to Flutterwave to complete your gift.

What your gift goes towards

₦1,000 Writing materials — roughly what one pupil needs to return to class
₦5,000 Materials for a trainee working through the six-week skill programme
₦25,000 A share of running one community edition of Ray of Hope
₦100,000 Meaningful support towards a full training cohort or a Back to School round

Amounts are indicative, not contractual. Funds are pooled across a programme rather than ring-fenced per gift, so we can direct money where the need is greatest in that cycle. Ask us for a breakdown any time — see the FAQ below.

Give your time

Volunteer with us

Our programmes are carried by people who show up. Some volunteer on the ground in Ibadan; some help from a laptop, anywhere.

Trainer or facilitator

Teach a craft, run a session, or help facilitate a Ray of Hope edition on the ground.

Mentor

Spend time with young people who need to see someone a few steps ahead of them.

Storyteller

Photography, video or social media — help us document the work honestly.

Logistics & ops

Move materials, set up an exhibition, coordinate a trade fair, keep records tidy.

Tell us how you'd like to help

We will get back to you by email. Nothing here is binding — it just starts a conversation.

Work with us

Partner with Chrysolite

We work with businesses, schools, faith groups, community associations and other non-profits. A partnership does not have to be a cheque — it can be a skill, a venue, a supply of materials, or a market for what our trainees make.

Ways to partner

  • Fund a cohort. Sponsor a full six-week training round and we will report back on who took part and what they made.
  • Donate materials. Fabric, yarn, crochet hooks, stationery, books, uniforms — the practical things a programme consumes.
  • Host an exhibition. Give our trainees a trade fair, a shelf, or a stall where their work can meet a buyer.
  • Send your team. Corporate volunteering days, mentoring sessions, or guest facilitators.
  • Buy from the shop. A simple, repeatable way to keep the training loop funded — see the shop.
