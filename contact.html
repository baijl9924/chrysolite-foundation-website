Need help?

Asking on behalf of a child

If you are a parent, guardian, teacher or community member asking for help with a child's education, please include as much of the following as you can in your message. It helps us assess quickly and honestly.

  • The child's age and current class or level
  • Where they are — the community and town
  • What the barrier is — school fees, uniform, books, materials, or something else
  • What has already been tried — family, school, community efforts
  • How to reach you — a phone number and a good time to call

Please be realistic about our size. We are a small foundation working through community referrals, and our Back to School rounds have a limited number of places. We will always tell you honestly whether we can help in the current round — and if we cannot, we will say so rather than leave you waiting.
