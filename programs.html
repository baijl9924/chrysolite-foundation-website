Our Programs

Four programmes, one chain: we meet young people, teach them something they can earn from, get their siblings back into school, and help what they make find a buyer.

Flagship programme

Ray of Hope Outreach

Ray of Hope is our flagship community outreach, now in its fourth edition. It runs over several weeks inside a single underprivileged community, combining encouragement with real, practical training.

Each edition closes with an exhibition, trade fair and awards ceremony. Trainees present what they have made, share what they learned, and — for many — earn their first income from something they created themselves. Parents and community members are invited to see it happen.

What a Ray of Hope edition involves

  • Multi-week training delivered inside the community
  • Mentoring sessions with volunteers and role models
  • Exhibition and trade fair to sell what trainees make
  • Awards and recognition for participants
  • Referral of children with urgent needs into Back to School

Skills

6-Week Skill Empowerment Programme

This is the practical heart of our work. Over six weeks, children and young adults in a partner community learn a craft from scratch — and leave with something they made and the ability to make more.

Our cohorts run in the Isale Osun community, Ibadan North East, Oyo State, open to children and young adults aged 5 to 27. Parents are involved throughout. The six weeks end with a public exhibition and a trade fair where the trainees sell what they made; the funds raised go towards an empowerment centre in the community and towards the next cohort.

Sessions are taught by foundation volunteers and specialist tutors. You can see who they are.

Skills we have taught

Crochet

Sling bags, scrunchies and small accessories, worked by hand.

Adire — tie & dye

Traditional Yoruba tie-dye and indigo textile work.

Ankara craft

Books and journals bound in ankara cloth.

Upcycling

Abandoned tyres rebuilt into stools and tables.

Enterprise basics

Finding buyers, pricing, and calculating profit so the business lasts.

Waste & environment

Seminars run with the sustainability NGO The AID NG on indiscriminate waste disposal.

Education

Back to School

Back to School is our direct education-support programme, now in its third edition. We work with families who have been forced to choose between school and survival, and we remove that choice by covering the practical costs of attendance.

What we cover

School fees, uniforms, textbooks, exercise books, writing materials and school bags.

When it runs

Concentrated around the start of each academic session, with follow-up through the term.

₦1,000

Puts one child back in class

Roughly what one pupil needs in writing materials to return to school. Every amount counts.

Campaign

Beyond the Borders

Beyond the Borders is the campaign under which we extend our reach past our immediate community. Need does not stop at a state line, and neither should our response to it.

Back to School 3.0 ran under this banner. As we grow, Beyond the Borders is how we take the model — outreach, training, education support — into new communities with local partners on the ground.

The full journey

How it fits together

  1. 01
    We meet them
    Ray of Hope brings us into a community and into contact with the young people who need support.
  2. 02
    We teach them
    Six weeks of skill training — crochet, adire, ankara craft — in their own community.
  3. 03
    We help them sell
    Trainees exhibit at a trade fair, and their products go into our online shop.
  4. 04
    We get their siblings into school
    Back to School covers fees, uniforms and materials for children who cannot afford them.
  5. 05
    It pays for itself
    100% of shop sales fund the next training cohort. The chain continues without us.
