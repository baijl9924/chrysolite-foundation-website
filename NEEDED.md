| The [CAC public search](https://search.cac.gov.ng/) for `7008093` | Entity type **IT**, name, `RC - 7008093`, date of registration, **status Active**, Tax ID | ✗ |

So the trustee list is not sitting in any source we can reach on our own. The two places it does
exist are:

- the foundation's **Form CAC 1.1** — the incorporation application, which names the trustees and
  which the foundation will have on file, or
- a **Certified True Copy** of the register entry from the CAC, which is a paid application

**And it is optional.** We want to be clear about that, because we pushed on this harder than it
deserves. The About page already publishes the certificate, the registration number, the status
and the search link — a donor can verify the organisation in under a minute without knowing a
single trustee's name. The team section names the seven people who actually do the work.

So: if the foundation is happy to publish the trustees, send the names and we will add them. If
it would rather keep them private, that is a perfectly good answer too, and we will simply leave
the page as it is. Either way nothing is blocked.

One thing we would still like, because it is cheap: **confirmation that the seven people in the
team section are the current line-up** (§A2).

### 1. The domain — the `.ng` names are free; `.com` and `.org` are not

**Checked 10 Oct 2026 against the registries directly (RDAP), not a reseller's search box.**

The `.com` and `.org` are both gone, and neither is usable. `.com` belongs to an **unrelated
charity of the same name in Zimbabwe**; `.org` was registered in August 2025 and lapsed in August
2026, and sits in the redemption period now. Both re-confirmed on 10 Oct 2026. There are five
other organisations trading under "Chrysolite" — the full list is below, and it is the reason the
extension alone will not tell a donor which Chrysolite they have found.

| Domain | Status 10 Oct 2026 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `chrysolitefoundation.ng` | **available** | the recommendation — already the address in the site's files |
| `chrysolitefoundation.org.ng` | **available** | non-profit extension; see recommendation |
| `chrysolitefoundation.com.ng` | **available** | cheapest option |
| `chrysolite.ng` / `chrysolite.org.ng` | **available** | short brand form |
| `chrysoliteempowerment.org.ng` | **available** | if the legal name is preferred in full |
| `chrysolitefoundation.com` | taken | Zimbabwe charity, via Wix |
| `chrysolitefoundation.org` | taken | registered Aug 2025, **expired Aug 2026**, now in the redemption period — not usable |
| `chrysolite.com.ng` | taken | |

**Recommended: `chrysolitefoundation.ng`**

The deciding criterion is recognition, not price: **someone seeing the address should know at
once which organisation it is.** Against five other organisations trading as "Chrysolite", only
two things do that work — the word *foundation* (which is the name people actually use for this
one) and `.ng` (which rules out the Zimbabwe charity on `.com`, the Baltimore mission on `.org`
and the Kenyan consultancy on `.com`).

It is also already the address in the site's own files, so there is nothing to reconcile.

| Pick | Price/yr | Length | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`chrysolitefoundation.ng`** | ~$8.91 / ₦13,250 | 25 | **primary — the name plus the country** |
| `chrysolite.ng` | ~$8.91 / ₦13,250 | 13 | short alias, redirects to the primary |
| `chrysolitefoundation.org.ng` | ~$9.00 | 27 | defensive; catches `.org` typists |
| `chrysolitefoundation.com.ng` | ~$3.63 / ₦6,250 | 27 | cheapest tier, if price ever matters again |

**Why not `.org.ng` for the primary.** *"foundation"* already carries the non-profit meaning, so
`chrysolitefoundation.org.ng` says it twice — and it is the `.ng` that actually separates this
organisation from the other Chrysolites, not the `org`. The About page carries the CAC number,
the **Active** status and the certificate; the address does not have to prove anything.

**Suggested bundle (~$27/yr ≈ ₦40,000).** `chrysolitefoundation.ng` as the address everywhere,
`chrysolite.ng` as a short form that redirects to it for business cards, WhatsApp and radio, and
`chrysolitefoundation.org.ng` held defensively so nobody else takes the obvious sibling. All three
are available. If the board wants one domain only, take the first.

**Why a bundle is worth it here.** "Chrysolite" is not a distinctive name on the internet — see
the table below. Holding the obvious neighbours is cheaper than a dispute later.

than typing, and because search will still find the site. Registering misspellings defensively is
not worth the money at this size.

**On price.** `.org.ng` and `.ng` cost the same, so the choice between them is about wording, not
money. `.com.ng` is the only genuinely cheaper tier — and it costs the full name in characters.

| Extension | Cheapest seen | Typical Nigerian registrar |
|---|---|---|
| `.com.ng` | ~$2.50–3.63 / yr | ₦6,250 / yr (MokoHost) |
| `.ng` | ~$7.38–8.91 / yr | ₦13,250 / yr (MokoHost) |
| `.org.ng` | ~$9.00 / yr | check in naira at checkout |

**No documents are required.** NiRA's own portal (`register.ng`) lists `.ng`, `.com.ng`,
`.org.ng`, `.net.ng`, `.i.ng`, `.name.ng` and `.mobi.ng` as **Open** zones — *"An open zone does
not require documentation, except in special cases."* Only `.edu.ng`, `.sch.ng`, `.gov.ng` and
`.mil.ng` are Closed. So `.org.ng` will not ask the foundation for proof of non-profit status.

**Where to buy — and the payment problem.** This depends on who is paying, and an earlier draft
of this section got it wrong by naming only Nigerian registrars.

- **Paying from inside Nigeria** → **TrueHost.com.ng** or **MokoHost**, both NiRA-accredited,
  priced in naira, payable by Nigerian card or bank transfer. `.ng` at MokoHost is ₦13,250.
- **Paying from outside Nigeria** → the Nigerian registrars above are effectively unusable; they
  expect a naira transfer or a Nigerian card, and the sites are often slow or unreachable from
  abroad. Use **Dynadot** instead: `.ng` at **$10.34** and `.org.ng` at **$10.25**, register /
  renew / transfer all at the same price, and it runs a Chinese-language storefront. International
  cards and PayPal are accepted.
- **Do not expect a mainland-Chinese registrar to work.** Aliyun and Tencent Cloud may only sell
  TLDs licensed by the regulator; a `.ng` domain is very unlikely to be on that list, so the
  option will simply not appear in their search. Registrars that do carry `.ng` in Chinese
  (AsiaRegister at ¥680/yr, SeaArea) charge roughly nine times the registry rate — usable, but
  only worth it if a domestic payment method is the hard requirement.
- **Avoid** resellers that mark `.ng` up by an order of magnitude — 101domain lists `.org.ng` at
  $124.99 and INWX at $118.80. Those are the same domain at twelve times the price.

**Do not trust a registrar's availability widget.** Registrars frequently show a name as taken
when the registry says otherwise, and several do not carry `.ng` sub-zones at all. The
authoritative check is NiRA's own RDAP server, which IANA lists as the `.ng` authority:

```
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" https://rdap.nic.net.ng/domain/<name>
```

`404` means unregistered (available); `200` means registered; **anything else is a failed request,
not a "taken"** — retry before drawing a conclusion. Verified this way on 10 Oct 2026, with
`nira.org.ng`, `google.com.ng` and `zenithbank.com.ng` as positive controls returning `200`.

**One warning about the cheap price.** `.com.ng` first-year prices of $2.50 are loss-leaders;
check the *renewal* price before committing. TrueHost's `.org.ng` is $9 first year **and** $9 on
renewal — no first-year bait — which is worth preferring even where the headline price is higher.

**What registering fixes.** The address appears **29 times across the ten shipped files** — eight
pages plus `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` — in `canonical` tags, `og:url`, `og:image`, the logo
reference and the sitemap. All of them currently point at a domain nobody owns, which is worse
than pointing nowhere. (A further 12 mentions live in the project's own `.md` files, which do not
ship.) Give us the name and we change all of them in one pass.

**If the recommendation is taken as-is, nothing needs changing** — the site already says
`chrysolitefoundation.ng`.
