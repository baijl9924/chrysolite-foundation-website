# What the site still needs from the foundation

**The site is finished and can go live today.** Donations, the shop link, the contact details
and all four forms work. Nothing below is a blocker — each item makes the site *accurate and
personal* rather than *generic*.

Items are ordered by how much they improve the site per unit of effort.

---

## A. Photographs — by far the biggest improvement

The site currently uses 14 stock photographs. They look right, but they are strangers. Replacing
them with the foundation's own pictures is the single change that turns this from "a nice
website" into "our website".

**How to send them:** original files, no screenshots, no WhatsApp compression if avoidable.
Anything over ~1600px wide is plenty. Keep the same orientation as the file it replaces — the
site crops to fit, so a portrait photo cannot fill a landscape slot.

|---|---|---|---|

## C. The trustees — four cards on the About page

Four cards currently read "Trustee name" over a silhouette. Named, accountable people are what
make a small non-profit credible. For each person we need:

1. **Full name** (as they want it published)
2. **Role** — e.g. Founder & Trustee, Programmes Lead, Volunteer Coordinator, Finance & Compliance
3. **A photograph** — head and shoulders, portrait orientation, against a plain background
4. Optional: one sentence of background, and a LinkedIn or email link

If there are only two or three people, say so — the layout adapts. **Do not invent names to fill
the four slots.** Fewer real names beat four invented ones.

## E. Two decisions

### 1. The domain — `chrysolitefoundation.ng` is available

The foundation ran its site there from February 2023 to February 2025. It lapsed and the domain
is now free to register. Registering it fixes several things at once: the `canonical` tags, the
social-share URLs and the sitemap currently point at a domain nobody owns.

- **Yes, register it** → we point the site at it and everything becomes correct
- **No, use something else** → tell us the name and we update the URLs

Note: `chrysolitefoundation.com` and `.org` both belong to an unrelated charity of the same name
in Zimbabwe. Do not use either.

### 2. How form submissions should reach the foundation

**As it stands**, the site already works with no setup: submitting a form opens the visitor's own
email app with the message ready to send to `chrysoliteinfo@gmail.com`. Nothing is lost, but the
visitor has to press send.

**Alternatively**, with a free Formspree account (or Netlify, if hosting there), submissions
arrive silently and land in the foundation's inbox directly. This is a better experience and
takes about ten minutes to set up. Say the word and we will wire it up.
